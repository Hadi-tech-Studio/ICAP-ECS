Subject-Verb Agreement Key Rules:
1. Singular subjects with "along with" take singular verbs.
2. In "Neither... nor", the verb agrees with the closer subject.
3. Collective nouns take singular verbs when acting together.
4. "Each" and "Everyone" strictly take singular verbs.
5. Inverted sentences put auxiliary verbs before the subject.
